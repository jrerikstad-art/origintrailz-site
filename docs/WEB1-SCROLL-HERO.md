# WEB.1 Scroll hero — walk the fog

**Status:** landing interaction is **scroll-driven reveal** on a frozen Bergura-A snapshot (terrain + semantics + mask texture).

## Design

- Scroll maps to **distance along a real road route** (not point index), driven by `.scroll-panels` height — not the whole document.
- Discovery is a **200×300 single-channel mask** at 10 m cells: `mix(paper, natural, revealMask)`. Scroll updates mask pixels only.
- Fog owner: `engine-mask-texture` (not canvas `#fog`, not `THREE.Fog`).
- Semantics load from `/snapshot/bergura-a-v1/world/semantic/` with schema normalize (`roads` or `features.roads`).
- Strict heights: `sampleHeight → null` skips the feature.
- **Wheel always scrolls** the page. Drag rotates. Pinch and visible `+ / −` zoom. Orbit reverses when scrolling back into the story.
- Opening seeds **~90 m** of reveal and eases the first quarter of progress faster.
- Renderer is **separate** from the app engine: preload only, frozen snapshot, never the live factory.
